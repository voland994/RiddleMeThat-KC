### The judgment call (the player's)

Real epileptologists disagree on this one:

- **(a) Hold at about six months.** Finish the crossover (roughly 10 to 12 weeks), then about three months seizure-free on levetiracetam alone before trying. The reasoning: JME can break through during a switch (hers did at 22, on lamotrigine), and being seizure-free for 9 to 12 months before pregnancy is associated with a good chance of staying seizure-free during it [exact figures from the EURAP registry, verify].
- **(b) Start trying once the valproate is fully out** (about three months). Valproate's risk to a pregnancy ends once it's gone, and levetiracetam has one of the lowest malformation rates of any seizure drug. The cost: if levetiracetam doesn't hold, she's pregnant and having seizures, and switching again during pregnancy is harder.
- **(c) A middle path.** Once the valproate is out, she waits another six to eight weeks on levetiracetam alone with no seizures and no morning jerks, and a levetiracetam level is checked as a pre-pregnancy baseline (levels drop in pregnancy, so a baseline is standard anyway).
- **Training lean:** Euclid would probably lean (a) or (c). Plenty of good community epileptologists would accept (b) or (c).
