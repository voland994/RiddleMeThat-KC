- **Fictional stand-in for Saint Luke's Marion Bloch Neuroscience Institute,** the neuroscience institute of a large nonprofit health system's flagship hospital in Kansas City, Missouri, near the Country Club Plaza. [verify details against the real institution before leaning on them]

## Kansas City, Missouri (proposed facts, [verify] where marked)

- **Size:** a city of about half a million, in a metro of over two million that straddles the Missouri–Kansas state line. Kansas City, Kansas, is a separate city across the line.
- **Getting around:** very car-dependent: wide highways, sprawl, parking lots. There's a **free downtown streetcar** (River Market to Union Station). The **Main Street extension** (to the Plaza and UMKC at 51st & Brookside) **opens Friday, October 24, 2025.** In August 2025 it's still under construction, so Main Street is torn up.
- **Neighborhoods (for choosing where he lives):** Crossroads (galleries, lofts, First Fridays), River Market (on the streetcar, older brick), Westport (bars), Midtown, Hyde Park (old houses, near the Plaza), Brookside (walkable little commercial strip), the Country Club Plaza (Spanish-style shopping district, near the hospital), Union Hill, Columbus Park.
- **Culture:** the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art (free admission [verify]), the Kemper Museum, the Kauffman Center for the Performing Arts (symphony, opera, ballet), the 18th & Vine jazz district, independent bookstores [verify names].
- **Climbing (real gyms):** Sequence (1710 Washington, edge of the Crossroads; bouldering, the best-rated in the city proper), RoKC (several locations incl. North KC), Apex (Overland Park, KS), Rendezvous (Leawood, KS; bouldering, opened April 2024). Whether to use real names or fictional stand-ins is still open.
- **Weather in late August:** hot and humid, often around 90°F, with evening thunderstorms.
- **Getting home:** Kansas City International Airport (MCI) has a new single terminal (opened 2023). Manitowoc is a long drive (about 600+ miles [verify]) or a connecting flight to Green Bay.
