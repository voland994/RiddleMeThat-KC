# Characters

Each person gets:

- **What they want:** something that has nothing to do with Philip.
- **Philip, to them:** how much they actually think about him, which is often not much.
- **Perceptiveness:** how much they read people, and whether they'd ever say it out loud.

## Wisconsin

### Lisa Sedlacek, about 58: Philip's mother (established)

- Still in Manitowoc, in the grandparents' house. Works in administration. Talks to Philip weekly, on the phone, and gets financial help from him.
- Her joke: he did it, and they're milking it for profit.
- **Her own life (proposed):** she has her own friends and routines, and her life doesn't revolve around her son. Possibly a serious birder (the Christmas Bird Count at Woodland Dunes in Two Rivers, a life list past 200). Philip knows she "watches birds" and doesn't know it's serious.
- **Perceptiveness:** knows his voice better than anyone, but reads it through her own worries. Says it rarely, and sideways.
- **Writing note:** go easy on Wisconsin signifiers (Packers, Culver's, "hon," "for Pete's sake"). A few are texture; stacked, they turn her into a type.

### Dale Hintz, early 60s: Lisa's partner of about seven years (established)

- A divorced mechanic who owns his own shop in Manitowoc. Drives the shop truck.
- **Philip, to him:** a bit impressive. "Lisa's son, the brain doctor."
- **Perceptiveness:** takes people at face value.

### The grandparents (established, deceased)

- Grandma: an elementary school teacher. Grandpa: a union worker at a plant in town. Philip grew up in their house.

### The father (established)

- Lives in Green Bay with the family he went back to. Never reached out. **No convenient reunions.**

### Anna Lindgren, about 33: the ex (name proposed)

## Cleveland (the past)

### Sana Qureshi: the friend he still texts (established)

- **From Lahore, Pakistan.** Trained with Philip at Euclid on a **J-1 visa.**
- **An epileptologist.** Getting a **Conrad 30 waiver** meant taking a job in an underserved area instead of going back to Lahore for the J-1's two years at home. The waiver requires three years full-time in an underserved area. She's now in **Charleston, West Virginia,** where she may be one of very few epileptologists for a large region.
- **Married to an engineer.** They're working toward a green card. **He's on a dependent (H-4) visa and not allowed to work yet,** so he's at home in Charleston. It takes a toll, even though they knew it was coming and are generally solid. (His name is open.)
- **With Philip:** the kinship continued. There's no romantic tension. **Proposed:** they text each other EEG screenshots.
- **Proposed timeline:** same residency class (2020–2023), one fellowship year (2023–24), and the Charleston job since summer 2024. That would make her a year ahead of him as an attending.
- **Open:** how they talk, and her perceptiveness.

### Everyone else

- His training cohort, co-residents, fellows, and attendings at Euclid exist but are **unnamed.** Build them only as the story needs them: a friend he still texts, a mentor who wrote his letters, whoever decided not to keep him.

## Kansas City

### Blue River Neuroscience Institute (established)

The parent system, **Meramec Health** (St. Louis), absorbed Blue River's system in 2024. Half the building is quietly dealing with that.

#### Dr. Doug Reinholt, 58: director of the epilepsy center
- Grew up in Salina, Kansas. Has run the program for 12 years.
- **Wants:** to keep his 12 EMU beds and grow SEEG while St. Louis talks "utilization metrics." About three years from stepping down.
- **Philip, to him:** filled the retirement gap. The Euclid SEEG training helps his case with St. Louis. Likes him, doesn't think about him much.
- **Perceptiveness:** medium. Reads people as resources and almost never comments on them.
- **Voice:** short sentences, talks in numbers (beds, wait times, days to first appointment).

#### Dr. Judith Hartsock, 67: the retired epileptologist (mostly offstage)
- 25 years at Blue River. Beloved. Kept long-stable patients on older drugs (phenytoin, phenobarbital). Some patients had her cell number.
- **Wants:** to actually be retired. Still answers some patient emails.
- Lives in charts, in patients' mouths ("Dr. Hartsock always..."), and maybe one coffee if Philip asks.

#### Denise Ortiz, APRN, 49: the epilepsy nurse practitioner
- Worked with Hartsock for 15 years. Grew up in the Westside.
- **Wants:** tuition for her son, a high school senior applying to colleges. Her own panel recognized under the new productivity model.
- **Philip, to her:** the fifth new doctor she's had to break in. She'll decide within about a month whether he listens.
- **Perceptiveness:** high. Almost never says what she reads. Shows it through which patients she sends him.
- **Voice:** brisk and practical. Knows patients' first names and their dogs' names. Warm, but not anyone's mom.

#### Dr. Eric Tran, 41: epileptologist and SEEG lead
- Trained at Mayo, grew up in Houston, two small kids. Runs surgery conference.
- **Wants:** to be the next director, with the SEEG program as his case.
- **Philip, to him:** a useful pair of hands. Possibly a future rival, which Eric hasn't thought about.
- **Perceptiveness:** low to medium. Too busy. Takes people at face value.
- **Voice:** fast, excited about technology, full of jargon.

#### Shawna Pruitt, RN, 38: EMU charge nurse
- Ten years on the unit. Grew up in Independence, Missouri. Community college, then a nursing degree.
- **Wants:** safe nurse staffing on the EMU. Chairs its patient-safety committee. Wants a nurse-educator role.
- **Philip, to her:** judged entirely by his orders. His explicit orders quietly earn points.
- **Perceptiveness:** sharp about competence, uninterested in feelings. Will say out loud when an order is bad.
- **Voice:** blunt and funny. Nothing like Denise.

#### Marcus Webb, 52: lead EEG technologist
- 25 years in the job. Knows EEG artifacts better than most neurologists.
- **Wants:** to retire in about three years with his team intact. Has heard St. Louis may centralize remote ICU EEG monitoring (proposed rumor).
- **Philip, to him:** judged by how he treats techs.
- **Perceptiveness:** medium. Reads people, keeps quiet.
- **Voice:** quiet and precise.

#### Dr. Tyler Brandt, PGY-3: neurology resident
- From Wichita, KU medical school, lives in Westport.
- **Wants:** a stroke fellowship. Epilepsy is a rotation to get through.
- **Philip, to him:** the new attending who makes him talk before giving the answer. Mildly annoying, for now.
- **Perceptiveness:** low.
- **Voice:** relaxed and competent.
