# Characters

Each person gets:

- **What they want:** something that has nothing to do with Philip.
- **Philip, to them:** how much they actually think about him, which is often not much.
- **Perceptiveness:** how much they read people, and whether they'd ever say it out loud.

## Kansas City

### Blue River Neuroscience Institute (established)

The parent system, **Meramec Health** (St. Louis), absorbed Blue River's system in 2024. Half the building is quietly dealing with that.

#### Dr. Doug Reinholt, 58: director of the epilepsy center
- Grew up in Salina, Kansas. Has run the program for 12 years.
- **Wants:** to keep his 12 EMU beds and grow SEEG while St. Louis talks "utilization metrics." About three years from stepping down.
- **Philip, to him:** filled the retirement gap. The Euclid SEEG training helps his case with St. Louis. Likes him, doesn't think about him much.
- **Perceptiveness:** medium. Reads people as resources and almost never comments on them.
- **Voice:** short sentences, talks in numbers (beds, wait times, days to first appointment).

#### Dr. Judith Hartsock, 67: the retired epileptologist (mostly offstage)
- 25 years at Blue River. Beloved. Kept long-stable patients on older drugs (phenytoin, phenobarbital). Some patients had her cell number.
- **Wants:** to actually be retired. Still answers some patient emails.
- Lives in charts, in patients' mouths ("Dr. Hartsock always..."), and maybe one coffee if Philip asks.
- **They haven't met.** She sent him a short, warm email with a spreadsheet of "patients to keep an eye on." **He replied.**

#### Denise Ortiz, APRN, 49: the epilepsy nurse practitioner
- Worked with Hartsock for 15 years. Grew up in the Westside.
- **Wants:** tuition for her son, a high school senior applying to colleges. Her own panel recognized under the new productivity model.
- **Philip, to her:** the fifth new doctor she's had to break in. She'll decide within about a month whether he listens.
- **Perceptiveness:** high. Almost never says what she reads. Shows it through which patients she sends him.
- **Voice:** brisk and practical. Knows patients' first names and their dogs' names. Warm, but not anyone's mom.
- **Home (established in cutaway):** a bungalow on the Westside, three blocks from where she grew up. Son **Gabe**, 17, a senior applying to KU Engineering. Judy Hartsock texts her every Monday asking about "my people."

#### Dr. Eric Tran, 41: epileptologist and SEEG lead
- Trained at Mayo, grew up in Houston, two small kids. Runs surgery conference.
- **Wants:** to be the next director, with the SEEG program as his case.
- **Philip, to him:** a useful pair of hands. Possibly a future rival, which Eric hasn't thought about.
- **Perceptiveness:** low to medium. Too busy. Takes people at face value.
- **Voice:** fast, excited about technology, full of jargon.

#### Shawna Pruitt, RN, 38: EMU charge nurse
- Ten years on the unit. Grew up in Independence, Missouri. Community college, then a nursing degree.
