# Precommit: the Hinge pool, late August 2025

**Written before play.** These are the women Philip sees in his first real week of swiping in Kansas City. The pool is fixed. It doesn't tilt toward whatever he turns out to like.

- **Settings assumed:** women aged 28 to 37, within about 10 miles of Roanoke. That reaches Johnson County in Kansas.
- **His profile, as assumed here:** sparse. "Physician," no workplace named. Good-enough photos. The player hasn't written his prompts or picked his photos yet. **If his real profile differs a lot from this, Claude says so out of character before playing any reaction that depends on it.**
- **Every condition triggers only on his outward actions:** his profile, his likes, his comments, and his messages. His thoughts never count.
- **Each woman has two layers.** "On the app" is what Philip sees. "Behind the profile" is hidden. **The player can skip the hidden layers until after the scene.**
