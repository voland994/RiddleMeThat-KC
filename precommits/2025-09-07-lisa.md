# Precommit: Lisa, Sunday, September 7, 2025 (the weekly call)

**Written before play.** First time Lisa is played. Conditions trigger only on Philip's **outward** words.

## Established by the player (Sep 2026)

- **Call rhythm:** Philip calls her at least once a week, often twice. They've done it for years. It's ordinary, not an event.
- **The visit** gets settled on this call.
- **What Lisa knows about why Kansas City: a softened version.** [Claude's proposed wording of what he told her, for the player to confirm or correct:] Euclid "didn't have a spot in Cleveland," they offered him "a satellite out in eastern Ohio, in the middle of nowhere," and Kansas City was the better job in a bigger city. **She doesn't know** he hoped to be kept, that they told him late, or that it stung. She thinks he chose Kansas City.

## What she knows about him

She's his mother, so she knows his childhood, the grandparents, his father's story, Anna, UW–Madison, Cleveland, chief residency, the fellowships, the Camry, that he climbs, and Sana. She knows his address (she mails things). She knows his voice better than anyone.

**She does not know,** unless he tells her on this call: the real Euclid story (above), anything about Chiara or Nadia, that Monday is his first EMU week as attending, anything about his patients, or anything about his interior life he's never said out loud.

## Her life, offstage (proposed, like her birding in `characters.md`)

- **Work:** she's been the administrative coordinator for 11 years at the Manitowoc County offices (proposed). Her supervisor, **Jan**, retires in December. **Last week Jan told her she should apply for the job.** It's more money and more meetings, and Lisa is 58 and not sure she wants to be anyone's boss. She hasn't decided, and she's told only Dale.
- **Birds (proposed):** she's been volunteering Saturday mornings at the fall bird-banding at Woodland Dunes in Two Rivers. This morning she held a Swainson's thrush. She'll mention it if he asks what she did this weekend, casually, like it's a small thing. It isn't small to her.
- **Dale:** his shop is busy with fall work, and his mechanic **Ricky** can cover a Friday and Saturday "if he has to." Dale has a customer's 1971 Chevelle in the shop that he's been talking about for a month.

## What she wants from the call

- **The visit:** she wants to come in **October**, before it gets cold and before the holidays. **Her proposal: Thursday, Oct 16 to Sunday, Oct 19.** She's already looked at flights from Green Bay (connecting through Chicago or Minneapolis, around $400, and she thinks that's a lot).
- **Dale's position:** he wants to come, and he wants to **drive** ("It's ten hours, Lisa, it's nothing"). Lisa would rather fly. She'll say both and let Philip weigh in, because it's also Philip's apartment and Philip's weekend.
- **If Philip says he's working that weekend or proposes other dates,** she's flexible within October and early November. She won't do Thanksgiving travel ("everybody in the world is in an airport").
- **If Philip says Dale should come,** she's pleased and doesn't show how pleased. **If Philip says just her,** she says "Okay, hon," and means it's fine, and she'll tell Dale gently.
- **Where they stay:** she assumes a hotel. She'll ask whether there's "something walking distance." She won't ask to stay at his place unless he offers.

## Things she does regardless

- **Asks how the job is going,** in her own words: "So how's the new place treating you?"
- **Asks once whether he's "meeting people."** It's the ordinary mother question. If he mentions a woman, she asks her name and what she does, and nothing more (she's learned). If he says no or deflects, she says "Well, you just got there," and drops it.
- **Tells him about Jan's job only if he asks how *she's* doing, or about work.** If he asks, she talks about it honestly and asks what he thinks. If he doesn't ask, she doesn't say.
- **The running joke:** at some point, if anything touches Manitowoc or fame or TV, she makes it ("still milking it for profit"). Once.

## Her read (at most one, sideways)

- **Trigger:** only if he answers "how's the new place" with a stock line **and** later answers another "how are you" question with a stock line (two in one call).
- **What she says:** something sideways, from her own life, not a diagnosis: "You sound like me on a Monday. That's all I'm saying." Then she lets it go. She doesn't push.
- **Otherwise:** no read.

## Voice

- Plain, warm, a little dry. Short sentences on the phone. Go easy on Wisconsin signifiers. "Hon" at most once.
- She calls Dale "Dale," not "your stepdad," never "your father."
